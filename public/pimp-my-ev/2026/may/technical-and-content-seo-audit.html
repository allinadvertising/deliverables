Proposed Solutions

Fix Shopify URL Consolidation and Parameter Crawling First

1
Emit canonical_url in <head> for all indexable templates using Shopify's native canonical tag to point to the clean product URL.
2
Convert all internal links to clean product URLs in product cards, recommendations, recently viewed, collection grids, and app snippets. Avoid collection-scoped product links.
3
Strip internal parameters including pr_prod_strat, pr_rec_id, pr_rec_pid, pr_ref_pid, pr_seq, _pos, _sid, _ss, aff, and utm_* from href values.
4
Add robots.txt.liquid disallows for non-content params only after clean links and canonicals are live, to preserve analytics while reducing crawl waste.

Normalize Sitemap and Orphan Handling

1
Keep parameterized URLs out of sitemaps : include only canonical products, collections, pages, and articles.
2
Reinstate internal links for eligible products by verifying Active status, Online Store channel, visible collection assignment, and hub links.
3
Rationalize legacy URLs : update internal sources for 301/404 orphans, and keep redirects only where a true successor exists.

Differentiate Product and Collection Content

1
Implement metafields for fitment and specs (model years, materials, finish, install notes, compatibility limits, package contents, warranty, FAQs).
2
Refactor templates to surface unique content first : intro, fitment, specs, and install notes should precede trust and recommendation modules.
3
Consolidate near-identical products : use variants for true options, not separate near-duplicate product pages.

Bulk Repair SEO Titles and Descriptions

1
Export and update resource.seo fields in bulk via Shopify admin, Matrixify, or Admin API.
2
Apply concise title formulas that lead with intent plus one differentiator (product type, vehicle model, material, or year range).
3
Rewrite meta descriptions to 120:155 characters with a product-specific differentiator. Do not reuse long body copy.

Repair Heading Hierarchy

1
Ensure H1 is first in the outline by moving Recently Viewed and similar modules below the main H1 or rendering them as non-heading elements.
2
Enforce exactly one H1 per template : map product H1s to product.title and collection H1s to collection.title.
3
Demote repeated utility labels like "IMPORTANT NOTE ABOUT THIS PRODUCT" and "Top Picks Similar to This Product" to paragraph/div elements.

Fix Structured Data Parsing

1
Remove the empty JSON-LD script from the affected product template or app embed.
2
Render JSON-LD conditionally and safely : output Product, ProductGroup, Offer, BreadcrumbList, Organization, and WebPage only when variables are populated and JSON-escaped.
3
Validate fixes in Google's Rich Results Test and Schema Markup Validator for representative product, collection, and discontinued templates.

Glossary: Technical Terms, Simplified

Canonical tag
A hint that tells search engines the preferred URL when multiple versions of a page exist.
Crawl budget
The amount of attention a crawler gives a site. Duplicate and parameter URLs can waste it.
Orphan URL
A page that is not linked from crawlable pages, making it hard for search engines to find.
JSON-LD
A format for structured data that helps search engines understand products, offers, and pages.
Near-duplicate
Pages with highly similar content or structure that reduce uniqueness and ranking potential.
301 redirect chain
When a URL redirects through multiple hops. Chains dilute signals and slow crawls.
H1 / H2 hierarchy
The ordered outline of headings on a page. A clear hierarchy improves comprehension.
CMS template
A reusable layout in the theme that renders content. Template bugs can scale issues sitewide.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should we fix first to unlock results fastest?

We recommend consolidating parameterized product URLs and restoring canonical tags, then removing the empty JSON-LD block. This sequence reduces duplicates, stabilizes indexing, and restores rich result eligibility within the same release window.

How soon can we expect impact after the P0 fixes?

Most sites see crawl cleanup within 2 to 4 weeks of shipping clean links and canonicals. Structured data enhancements can surface in the same timeframe once Google recrawls key product templates.

Which tasks belong to AIA vs Client Dev?

AIA will guide linking rules, metadata formulas, and prioritization. Client Dev owns theme updates, canonical output, robots.txt.liquid, JSON-LD removal, and sitemap settings. We will provide QA test cases and validate live pages before and after launch.

Do we need to remove pages to reduce duplication?

We recommend consolidating true variants under a single product with options. Parameterized and collection-scoped product URLs should be de-parameterized and canonicalized, not indexed as separate pages.
