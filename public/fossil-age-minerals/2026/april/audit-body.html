Glossary: Technical Terms, Simplified

CTR (Click‑Through Rate)
The percentage of impressions that result in a click. Often improved by better titles/meta and richer SERP features.
Core Update
A broad Google algorithm update that can reassess content quality and relevance, affecting rankings across many queries.
Collection/Category Page
A Shopify page that lists grouped products. Critical for demand capture on high‑intent commercial queries.
GTM (Google Tag Manager)
A tag management system that centralizes tracking code, reducing duplicate loads and governance risk.
DataLayer
A structured object used to pass clean ecommerce and interaction data to analytics platforms like GA4 via GTM.
Sitemap
An XML file that lists site URLs for search engines. Failures (e.g., 500 status) can hinder discovery and indexing.
Near‑Duplicate Content
Pages with highly similar copy that dilute uniqueness and can weaken rankings across related collections.
Query Mix
The composition of search queries that surface a site. Shifts in mix can change CTR and average position without a sitewide rank crash.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should we fix first to regain clicks?
We recommend addressing two items in parallel: refresh and retarget the dinosaur and tyrannosaur collections for SERP intent, and normalize analytics by consolidating to a single tag load path. This restores click share where demand exists and ensures the data is trustworthy as changes roll out.

How long until we see results from the metadata and content refresh?
CTR improvements can surface within 1–3 weeks as Google recrawls. Ranking stability and impression gains typically require 3–8 weeks depending on crawl cadence and competitive pressure. Sitemap and internal link improvements help accelerate this timeline.

Which team owns each workstream?
AIA will lead SERP research, metadata testing, and internal linking plans. Client Dev will handle theme edits, tag consolidation (or GTM deployment), sitemap troubleshooting with Shopify, and form event instrumentation. We will coordinate QA and GSC/GA4 validation together.

Do we need to remove or noindex any pages?
Based on the audit, removals are not required. The priority is to enhance collection quality, refine query targeting, and fix the sitemap error. We will flag any thin or overlapping templates if future analysis shows persistent cannibalization.
